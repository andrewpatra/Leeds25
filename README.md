#Tune in to 25
 
For the 25th Anniversary of the naming gift for the Leeds School of Business, this installation was created to tap into Leeds and explore our archives. I wanted to create an installation for our community to interact with their history, one click at a time. This installation is built with a custom 3D printed dial featuring the 25th Anniversary mark, a Raspberry Pi, and some custom code that gives users a way to get "hands on" with the project.
 
Thanks to the Leeds Advancement team, Nikki Luth, Carolyn Gleason, and John Helmers for letting us experiment, to Erik Jeffries, Claire Stewart, and Inna Korikhman for allowing me to collaborate with the team and create, and finally to the Leeds Family who's gift made this possible.
